# P3: Routines v2 and proactive follow-up

User-visible changes, for the integrator to merge into `CHANGELOG.md` `[Unreleased]`.

## Added
- **Time zone.** Settings › Account has a time zone (`settings.timezone`, IANA). It starts at the hub's
  system zone (UTC on most VPSes) and offers this device's zone in one click. Routines are scheduled on
  this clock, so "daily 09:00" in Asia/Manila fires at 09:00 Manila on a UTC server. Changing it
  reschedules every routine.
- **One-shot routines.** A routine can fire once at a date and time (`onceAt`), then switches itself off.
  A one-shot the hub was down for fires at the next start. "Run now" does not use one up.
- **Schedule presets.** The Routines tab picks Once / Every day / Weekdays / Every week / Every month /
  Every year plus a time (and date, weekday or day of the month) and writes the cron for you. Raw cron
  is under "Advanced" (or "Custom (cron)"). The list shows schedules in words, next runs in your zone,
  and run history with status, duration and cost, and it refreshes when a routine changes or fires.
- **Bots can schedule routines.** New hub tools `create_routine`, `update_routine` and `delete_routine`
  (cron or `once_at`, in your time zone) always show an approval card first, like other fleet changes.
  `list_routines` is read-only and silent.
- **Turns survive a restart.** A turn that was queued or running when the hub stopped (or crashed) is
  resumed on the next start, with a note to the bot that it was cut off. Each turn is resumed at most
  once, so a turn that brings the hub down does not loop. A routine run left "running" is closed.
- **Stalled handoffs get one nudge.** If a bot does not answer a handoff within 10 minutes
  (`POCKETROCKET_HANDOFF_NUDGE_MIN`) while it is idle, the room's coordinator (or the bot that handed
  off) gets one follow-up turn to decide what to do. The nudge is a hop on the same thread, so the hop
  limit and the thread cost cap still apply, and each handoff is nudged at most once.

## Changed
- **Routine turns always ask.** A turn started by a routine, and every bot hop it causes, goes through
  approval cards even when approvals are bypassed in Settings or by `POCKETROCKET_BYPASS_PERMISSIONS`
  (decision Q6). Nobody is watching a 3 a.m. routine.
- REST: `POST/PATCH /api/routines` accept `onceAt` (epoch ms or local `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm`, read in
  `settings.timezone`; a past time is refused) and `cron` may be empty for a one-shot. `Routine` gains
  `onceAt`. `POST /api/routines/:id/run` no longer consumes a one-shot.
- WS: new `routines.changed` event (refetch `GET /api/routines`).

## Not user-visible
- Migrations 30 (`routines.once_at`) and 31 (`lane_journal`), registered as the P3 block `[30, 39]`.
- `services/FollowUp.ts`; `RoomRouter.requiresApprovals / freeze / isBusy / resume / followUp`;
  `TurnRequest.strictApprovals` and `PermCtx.strictApprovals` (`PermissionBroker.bypassedFor`).
- `@pocketrocket/shared` `schedule.ts`: preset ↔ cron helpers used by the Routines tab.
- The P0 migration-runner test now runs against the P0 migrations only (it used version 30 as a fake).

## Known limits
- Stalled-handoff watches live in memory: a handoff that was waiting when the hub restarted is not nudged.
- OpenCode bakes its permission config in at server spawn, so under bypass an OpenCode routine turn is
  only gated through the hub's own tools and `request_approval` (OpenCode is disabled in v1).
