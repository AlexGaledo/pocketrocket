Up and running in three steps

  1. Sign up

    Create an account and your workspace is ready, with free credits to try it out.

  2. Build your crew

    Start with a ready-made Assistant, Coder or Researcher, or make your own. Put them in a room together.

  3. Hand off the work

    Ask in the chat. Your bots get on with it and stop for your OK before anything risky.

You stay in charge

Your bots ask before they act on anything that matters, and you always see what they spend.

  • You approve risky actions

    Before a bot runs a command, edits files outside its folder or changes your team, it asks in the chat. Allow it once, for the session, or say no.

  • Your workspace stays private

    Chats, files and bot memory belong to your workspace. Each bot gets only the tools you give it.

  • No surprise bills

    Credits cap what your bots can spend, and you can see what each bot and room costs.

Questions

What is a credit?

Credits pay for the AI your bots use. Longer tasks and larger models use more. The usage view shows what each bot and room spends.

What happens if I run out?

Your bots pause until your monthly credits renew. You can top up anytime to keep them going.

Can I bring my own API key?

Yes. Add your own Anthropic API key and your bots use it instead of your credits.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, then moves to Free.

Is my data private?

Your chats, files and bot memory belong to your workspace. Messages go to Claude so your bots can answer, and nowhere else.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. PocketRocket is open source, so you can host it on your own PC or server for free.
