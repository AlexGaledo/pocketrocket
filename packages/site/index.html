Up and running in three steps

  1. Install

    Download the app and sign in with your Claude account. It takes about five minutes.

  2. Build your crew

    Start with a ready-made Assistant, Coder or Researcher, or make your own. Put them in a room together.

  3. Hand off the work

    Ask in the chat. Your bots get on with it and stop for your OK before anything risky.

You stay in charge

Your bots ask before they act on anything that matters, and your data stays with you.

  • You approve risky actions

    Before a bot runs a command, edits files outside its folder or changes your team, it asks in the chat. Allow it once, for the session, or say no.

  • Your keys stay on your machine

    Chats, files, memory and keys live on your PC or your own server. No telemetry.

  • No surprise bills

    Every conversation has a spending cap, and you can see what each bot and room costs.

How PocketRocket keeps you safe

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. PocketRocket is free and open source. Your bots run on your own Claude subscription or API key.

What do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC and a Claude Pro or Max plan, or an Anthropic API key. On a Mac or Linux you can run it from source.

Which AI does it use?

Claude. Bots use Sonnet by default, and you can switch any bot to Opus or Haiku.

Where does my data go?

Messages go to Claude so your bots can answer. Your chats, files and memory stay on your own machine or server.

Can a bot do something I didn't approve?

By default, risky actions wait for your OK. As with any new hire, give each bot only the access its job needs.

Can it keep working when my PC is off?

Yes. Run it on your own Linux server and connect from the desktop app. Routines keep running around the clock.
