# Code of conduct

Be respectful. Disagree about code, not people. No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory
language in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project space.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues/PRs, or block contributors who don't follow this.

Report a problem by emailing the maintainer listed on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/AlexGaledo),
or via a private message on the repository. Reports are handled discreetly.
